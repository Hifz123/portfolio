E-COMMERCE · SALES & CUSTOMER ANALYTICS

E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on evaluating e-commerce sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer activity, returns, regional performance and payment behavior.

Raw transactional data was cleaned and explored using Python and Pandas, analyzed using PostgreSQL and SQL, and transformed into an interactive Power BI dashboard for business-focused reporting and decision-making.

01 Sales Performance

Analyzed revenue, orders, quantity sold, average order value and monthly revenue trends to evaluate overall sales performance.

02 Product & Profitability

Compared revenue and profit across product categories to identify the strongest contributors to business performance.

03 Returns & Customers

Evaluated customer volume and return rates across categories to identify areas requiring further investigation.

04 Regional & Payment Analysis

Compared revenue across regions and payment methods to identify differences in customer purchasing behavior.

05 Business Finding

Generated $5.87M in revenue with $970.02K in profit and an overall profit margin of 16.5%.

06 Business Finding

Electronics was the strongest revenue and profit-generating category, followed by Home and Sports.

LEARNING ANALYTICS · ENGAGEMENT & OUTCOMES

Online Learning Engagement & Completion Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding student engagement, assessment performance, course outcomes, pass rates and withdrawal patterns using the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD).

Data was cleaned and prepared using Excel, analyzed using SQL in PostgreSQL, and transformed into an interactive Power BI dashboard to identify meaningful engagement patterns and generate business-oriented recommendations.

01 Student Outcomes

Analyzed pass and withdrawal rates to understand overall student outcomes across the dataset.

02 Engagement Analysis

Compared learning engagement across courses and student outcome groups to identify meaningful differences in activity levels.

03 Course Performance

Compared pass and withdrawal rates across course modules to identify courses requiring closer investigation.

04 Assessment Analysis

Evaluated assessment performance across different assessment types to identify variations in average scores.

05 Engagement Finding

Higher-engagement students showed a substantially higher pass rate than lower-engagement students in the analysis.

06 Course Finding

Withdrawal rates varied across courses, highlighting potential areas for further investigation and learner-support initiatives.
